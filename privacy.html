Privacy Policy

How Bestbite Enterprise collects, uses and protects customer information.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Bestbite Enterprise (“Bestbite”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect when you use our website, place an order, contact us or use our payment and ordering services.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, phone number, email address when provided, delivery address, order items, notes and order totals.
  • Payment information: online payments are processed by our payment provider, Paystack. We do not ask customers to send card PINs, passwords or secret payment credentials to Bestbite.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Website information: basic technical information may be processed by our hosting, database and security services to operate the website.
  • Local storage: the website may store cart and order-related information in your browser so that your shopping experience works properly. You can clear this through your browser settings.

2. How we use your information

We use customer information to receive and prepare orders, arrange delivery, process payments, provide order status updates, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse and improve our website and services.

3. Payment processing

If you choose online payment, payment processing is handled through Paystack. Payment details are submitted to the payment provider through the secure payment flow. Bestbite receives payment and transaction information needed to confirm your order.

4. WhatsApp ordering

If you choose to send an order through WhatsApp, the order details you choose to send are shared with Bestbite through WhatsApp so that we can confirm and prepare the order. WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy terms may also apply.

5. Service providers

We may use trusted technology providers, including our hosting provider, database provider and payment provider, to operate the website. They may process information only as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own terms and policies.

6. How long we keep information

We keep order and customer information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, accounting, security, dispute handling and legitimate business records. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with an order, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask questions about how your information is used. We may need to verify an order or customer request before making changes.

8. Children

Our ordering service is intended for customers who can legally enter into transactions. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children for independent purchasing.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so customers should avoid sending passwords, card PINs, OTPs or other secret credentials through chat or email.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
