HELP & SUPPORT

How can we help?

How do I place an order?

Choose items from the Menu, add them to your cart, enter your delivery details and submit the order by WhatsApp or email.

How much is delivery?

Delivery within Minna is between ₦1,500 and ₦2,000 depending on the delivery area selected.

How do I pay?

Online payment is not enabled for now. After submitting your order, the website displays the SweetBite transfer account details.

Can I request something not listed?

Yes. Use the Special Request box on the Menu or the special-request field at checkout.
